**What I changed:**
- **`src/paylink/invoice.py`**

- **`src/paylink/retry.py`**
  - The loop ran `attempts - 1` times, so it always made one call too few.
  - When every attempt failed, it returned `None` instead of raising the last error.
  - It now makes all `attempts` calls, raises the last exception if they all fail, and doesn't sleep after the final failure.
