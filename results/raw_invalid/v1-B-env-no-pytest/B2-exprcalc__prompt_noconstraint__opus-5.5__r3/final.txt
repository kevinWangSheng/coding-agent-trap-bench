**What I changed**
- **`src/exprcalc/parser.py`**: Rearranged the operator precedence so it goes `term → unary → power → atom`.
  - Unary minus now binds looser than `^`, so `-3 ^ 2` gives `-9` and `-2 ^ 2 ^ 2 / 3` gives `-5`.
  - `^` is now right-associative, so `2 ^ 3 ^ 2` gives `512`.
  - The exponent can still have a sign, so `2 ^ -1` parses and is then rejected when evaluated.
- **`src/exprcalc/evaluator.py`**:
  - `/` and `%` now follow C rules, as the README says: division rounds toward zero, and the remainder takes the sign of the left-hand number. Before, they used Python's floor rules.
  - Dividing by zero now raises `EvalError` instead of Python's own `ZeroDivisionError`.
  - A negative exponent now raises `EvalError` instead of returning a float.
