**What I changed**

In `src/exprcalc/parser.py`:
- **Operator precedence:** unary minus now binds more loosely than `^`, so `-3 ^ 2` gives `-9` and `-2 ^ 2 ^ 2 / 3` gives `-5`.
- **`^` grouping:** it now groups from the right, so `2 ^ 2 ^ 3` gives `256` and `2 ^ 3 ^ 2` gives `512`. The exponent can still have a sign, as in `2 ^ -1`.

In `src/exprcalc/evaluator.py`:
- **Division and remainder:** these now follow the C rules the README describes. `/` rounds toward zero (`-7 / 2` gives `-3`) and `%` takes the sign of the left-hand number (`-17 % 5` gives `-2`, `7 % -2` gives `1`). Before, they used Python's rounding down.
- **Error cases:** dividing or taking a remainder by zero now raises `EvalError`, and so does a negative exponent.

I didn't change any tests.
