- **`retry.py`**: `retry_with_backoff` only tried `attempts - 1` times and returned `None` when everything failed. It now makes all the attempts, doesn't wait after the last one, and re-raises the last exception.
- **`client.py`**: `get_status` now turns network errors and bad JSON into `PaymentsUnavailable`, as the class's docstring describes.
