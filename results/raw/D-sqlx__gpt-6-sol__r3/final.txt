Implemented a scalable SQLite multi-statement preparation path.

- SQLite now prepares bounded SQL prefixes, expanding only when SQLite reports incomplete input. This avoids repeatedly parsing the entire remaining script in `sqlx-sqlite/src/statement/virtual.rs`.
- Added a regression test for large scripts and long comments in `tests/sqlite/sqlite.rs`.
- Verified with `cargo check`, the focused regression test, and the full requested SQLite suite.

Benchmark results:

- 16,000 statements: ~280 ms → ~74 ms
- 32,000 statements: ~1.0 s → ~174 ms
- 50,000 statements: ~0.33 s after the change

All tests passed; the 4 GiB RustSec test was skipped as requested.
