**What I changed**

In `src/exprcalc/parser.py` I fixed operator precedence:
- A leading minus now applies after `^`, so `-3 ^ 2` is `-9`.
- `^` now groups right to left, so `2 ^ 3 ^ 2` is `512`.

In `src/exprcalc/evaluator.py` I made the maths match the C-style rules in the README:
- `/` rounds toward zero, so `-7 / 2` is `-3`.
- The result of `%` takes the sign of the left number, so `-17 % 5` is `-2`.
- Dividing by zero, with either `/` or `%`, now raises `EvalError` instead of Python's own `ZeroDivisionError`.
- A negative exponent, like `2 ^ -1`, now raises `EvalError`.
