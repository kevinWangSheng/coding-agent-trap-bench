Implemented scalable SQLite multi statement preparation.

- Bounded each `sqlite3_prepare_v3()` input to 4 KiB, growing only when SQLite reports an incomplete token/statement.
- Added regression coverage for large quoted values containing semicolons.
- Before: 32,000 statements took ~1.1 s.
- After: ~190 ms; 16,000 statements ~81 ms.
